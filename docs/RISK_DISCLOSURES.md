# Meridian Protocol (MRDN) — Risk Disclosures

This document is a technical risk disclosure draft, not legal advice and not a solicitation to buy tokens.

## Smart-contract risk

Source verification is complete on BscScan, but no independent Solidity review or formal audit is included in the supplied materials. Bugs in the contract, compiler toolchain, wallets, explorers, integrations, or user interfaces could cause loss.

The recorded initial holder received the full supply at deployment. Until allocation transfers, vesting, custody, and treasury controls are publicly evidenced, holders should treat supply concentration as a material risk.

## Market and liquidity risk

No liquidity provision, market-making arrangement, exchange listing, price, yield, return, or profit is evidenced by this package. Tokens may be illiquid or have no market.

## Legal and regulatory risk

No jurisdiction-specific legal or tax opinion is included. Token treatment varies by jurisdiction and facts. Qualified local counsel should review the project before any offering, marketing, sale, or distribution.

## Operational and dependency risk

The project may depend on RPC providers, explorers, wallets, exchanges, liquidity venues, hosting, social channels, and third-party software. Outages, censorship, phishing, key loss, or compromise can impair access.
